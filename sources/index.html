This page lists what each guide on this site actually cites, and how strong that source is. Nepal's national symbols are set partly by law and partly by custom, so the strength of the source changes what we can say.

How We Rate Sources

LevelWhat it isWhat we do with it
PrimaryThe Constitution, Nepal Gazette notices, a ministry or the issuing institutionState the fact and cite the Article or document where possible
News reportA newspaper report of a government decisionState the decision, name the report and flag anything we could not confirm
SecondaryEncyclopaedias, books, tourism boards, specialist sitesUse for background, not for "official" claims
Our rule. We call a symbol "official" only when we have seen the law or a report of the government decision. Otherwise we say "no official declaration confirmed". The full breakdown is on the Official vs Unofficial page.

The Main Legal Source

The Constitution of Nepal (2015) names the flag (Article 8), anthem and coat of arms (Article 9(1) and (2)), and the national flower, colour, animal and bird (Article 9(3)). Articles 6 and 7 cover language. Read it at: Constitution of Nepal 2015 (Constitute Project).

Before citing a symbol as official in an exam, thesis or news story, check the Constitution, the Nepal Gazette or the ministry concerned. This site is educational and is not a substitute for them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does symbols.thenepal.io get its information?

From the Constitution of Nepal where a symbol is named in law, from reports of government decisions, and from secondary sources such as encyclopaedias and specialist sites. Each guide lists its sources, and this page summarises them.

Which source is best for official national symbols?

The Constitution of Nepal (2015), Articles 6 to 9, together with the Nepal Gazette and the relevant ministry. Use those when you need an official citation.

Why do some guides say 'no official declaration confirmed'?

Because we could not find a law or government decision for them. It means unconfirmed, not that none exists.

How do I report an error or suggest a source?

Use the contact page on thenepal.io and include the page address and the source.
