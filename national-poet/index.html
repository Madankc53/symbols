Nepal's national poet (Rastra Kavi, राष्ट्रकवि) is Madhav Prasad Ghimire (1919-2020). The government conferred the title on him in 2003, according to the Kathmandu Post.

Who Was Madhav Prasad Ghimire?

Born23 September 1919, Putsun, Lamjung
Died18 August 2020, Lainchaur, Kathmandu, aged 101
TitleRastra Kavi (national poet), conferred by the government in 2003
Posts heldEditor of Gorkhapatra from 1946; chancellor of the Royal Nepal Academy (now Nepal Academy), 1988-1990

Source: The Kathmandu Post, "Madhav Prasad Ghimire, the Rastra Kavi, dies at 101" (19 August 2020).

His Best-Known Works

GauriKinnar KinnariCharu CharchaShakuntalaMalati MangaleRitambhara

Gauri is an epic written as an elegy for his first wife. Malati Mangale is a musical drama first staged in 1985. Ritambhara, his last epic, was unfinished when he died. Poems such as Nepali Hami Rahaula Kaha and Himal Pari Himal Wari are among those the Post lists as notable.

Source: The Kathmandu Post, 19 August 2020.

Is "National Poet" an Official Symbol?

It is an honorary title, not a constitutional symbol. The Constitution of Nepal fixes the flag, anthem, emblem, colour, flower, animal and bird. The Rastra Kavi title is a government honour given to a person. We have not seen the 2003 decision text, so the year rests on the Kathmandu Post report.

Ghimire was cremated with state honours in August 2020.

Source: The Kathmandu Post photo story on his final rites (19 August 2020).

✦ Did you know?

  • Ghimire lived to 101 and edited the state daily Gorkhapatra from 1946.
  • The national anthem's lyrics were not written by him. See our national anthem page for its lyricist and history.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the national poet of Nepal?

Madhav Prasad Ghimire (1919-2020) is Nepal's national poet, known as Rastra Kavi. The government conferred the title in 2003, according to the Kathmandu Post.

What does Rastra Kavi mean?

Rastra Kavi (राष्ट्रकवि) means national poet. It is an honorary title given by the government.

When did Madhav Prasad Ghimire die?

He died on 18 August 2020 at his home in Lainchaur, Kathmandu, aged 101.

What is Madhav Prasad Ghimire famous for?

He is known for epics and poems such as Gauri, Shakuntala and Ritambhara, the essay collection Charu Charcha and the musical drama Malati Mangale.

Is the national poet written in the Constitution of Nepal?

No. It is an honorary government title, not one of the symbols set by the Constitution.
