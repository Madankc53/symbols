Nepali New Year 2084 — 14 April 2027

Baisakh 1, 2084 BS falls on Wednesday, 14 April 2027 — the first day of the Bikram Sambat year, a public holiday in Nepal marked by family gatherings, greetings and festivals such as Bhaktapur's Bisket Jatra.

Nepal does not welcome the new year on 1 January. It begins on Baisakh 1, the first day of the first month of the Bikram Sambat calendar, which falls in mid-April. For 2084 BS that day is Wednesday, 14 April 2027.

The Date, Confirmed

Baisakh 1, 2084 BS = Wednesday, 14 April 2027. Panchang tables for Kathmandu list this day as Mesha Sankranti, the day the sun enters Aries and the Bikram Sambat year turns over.
Still to be confirmed. The Government of Nepal publishes its official public-holiday list for each year. We have not checked the final 2084 list, so confirm the holiday schedule with an official source before you plan travel or business around it.

Source: vedicmarga.com panchang for 14 April 2027 (Baishakh 1, 2084 BS, Kathmandu); OfficeHolidays.com, "Nepali New Year", which describes it as a national holiday in Nepal.

Why It Falls on 13 or 14 April

Bikram Sambat is a solar calendar: each year starts when the sun transits into Aries (Mesha Sankranti). That moment lands on 13 or 14 April in the Gregorian calendar, so the new year moves between those two dates from year to year rather than staying fixed.

Why the year number is so high. Bikram Sambat runs about 56 years and 8 months ahead of the Gregorian calendar. Between mid-April and December, add 57 to the Gregorian year to get the BS year (April 2027 is 2084); from January to mid-April, add 56 (January 2027 is still 2083).

Source: OfficeHolidays.com, "Nepali New Year" (April 13 or 14; 56 years and 8 months); see also our National Calendar guide.

How Nepal Celebrates

Baisakh 1 is a public holiday. Families gather, people exchange greetings and good wishes, and many take part in rituals meant to bring good fortune in the year ahead. Offices and schools close, and cities such as Kathmandu, Patan and Bhaktapur fill with visitors and local events.

Greetings: नयाँ वर्षको शुभकामनाFamily gatheringsTemple visitsBisket Jatra, Bhaktapur

Bisket Jatra. In Bhaktapur, the multi-day Bisket Jatra festival is timed around the new year, with processions and chariots in the old town. The exact festival dates for 2027 depend on the lunar-solar calendar, so check the local schedule closer to the time.

✦ Did you know?

  • The calendar is named for King Vikramaditya of Ujjain, according to the tradition OfficeHolidays.com records.
  • Nepal's new year is a solar new year; Newar communities also keep Nepal Sambat, which begins in autumn around Tihar, and many communities have their own Lhosar new years in winter.
  • Bikram Sambat is Nepal's official calendar, and the year number changes on Baisakh 1, not on 1 January.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Nepali New Year 2084?

Baisakh 1, 2084 BS falls on Wednesday, 14 April 2027, according to the Kathmandu panchang.

Is Nepali New Year a public holiday?

Yes. It is observed as a national holiday in Nepal. Check the Government of Nepal's official holiday list for the final schedule.

Why does Nepal's new year fall in April?

Bikram Sambat is a solar calendar whose year starts at Mesha Sankranti, when the sun enters Aries, usually on 13 or 14 April.

What year is it in Nepal now?

From mid-April 2026 until Baisakh 1 (14 April 2027) the Bikram Sambat year is 2083. From 14 April 2027 it becomes 2084.

How many years is Bikram Sambat ahead of the Gregorian calendar?

About 56 years and 8 months. Add 57 to the Gregorian year between mid-April and December, and 56 from January to mid-April.

What is Bisket Jatra?

A multi-day festival in Bhaktapur held around the Nepali New Year, with processions and chariots in the old town. Check local announcements for the exact 2027 programme.

How do you say Happy New Year in Nepali?

नयाँ वर्षको शुभकामना (Naya barsha ko shubhakamana). For 2084 people often write नयाँ वर्ष २०८४ को हार्दिक मंगलमय शुभकामना.

Does every community in Nepal celebrate on Baisakh 1?

Baisakh 1 is the national new year, but several communities keep their own calendars and new years, such as Nepal Sambat among the Newars and the various Lhosar festivals.
