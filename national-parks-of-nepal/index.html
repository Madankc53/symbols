Chhayanath: The 13th National Park

The government decided in 2025 to create Chhayanath National Park in Mugu district, Karnali Province, on a proposal from the Ministry of Forests and Environment. It covers about 843 km² and was carved out of Shey Phoksundo National Park, which is why Shey Phoksundo's area is now listed as 2,712 km² instead of the older 3,555 km². Wikipedia gives its establishment date as 29 August 2025, while some other lists show early September 2025, so we say "2025" in the table.

Why you still see "12 national parks". Shuklaphanta and Parsa were upgraded from wildlife reserves to national parks in 2017, which made 12. Chhayanath made 13. Older exam guides and travel sites have not all been updated.

Source: Himalayan Tribune, "Creation of Chhayanath National Park elates locals" (6 September 2025); Wikipedia, Chhayanath National Park.

Which Parks Are World Heritage Sites?

Two of the national parks are UNESCO World Heritage Sites: Sagarmatha (1979) and Chitwan (1984). See all four Nepali sites on our World Heritage Sites page. For the park that protects the highest mountain, read about Mount Everest. For the animal numbers behind the parks, see the 2026 tiger census, the rhino count and the snow leopard estimate.

✦ Did you know?

  • Chitwan, set up in 1973, is Nepal's oldest national park.
  • Shivapuri Nagarjun is the national park closest to Kathmandu, near enough for a day trip.
  • Five of the parks are in the Terai lowlands: Chitwan, Bardiya, Banke, Parsa and Shuklaphanta. They hold Nepal's wild tigers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many national parks are there in Nepal?

Nepal has 13 national parks. The 13th, Chhayanath National Park in Mugu district, was created in 2025. Before that Nepal had 12.

What are the 13 national parks of Nepal?

Chitwan, Sagarmatha, Langtang, Rara, Khaptad, Shey Phoksundo, Bardiya, Makalu Barun, Shivapuri Nagarjun, Banke, Shuklaphanta, Parsa and Chhayanath.

Which is the first national park of Nepal?

Chitwan National Park, established in 1973, is Nepal's first national park.

Which is the largest national park in Nepal?

Shey Phoksundo National Park, at about 2,712 km² after Chhayanath was carved out of it in 2025.

Which is the smallest national park in Nepal?

Rara National Park, at about 106 km².

Which is the newest national park of Nepal?

Chhayanath National Park in Mugu district, created in 2025.

Which national parks of Nepal are World Heritage Sites?

Sagarmatha National Park (inscribed 1979) and Chitwan National Park (1984).

Which national park is near Kathmandu?

Shivapuri Nagarjun National Park, established in 2002, lies on the northern edge of the Kathmandu Valley.
