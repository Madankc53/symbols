Nepal marks five major national days, each on a fixed Bikram Sambat (BS) date: Republic Day (Jestha 15), National Day (Ashoj 3), National Unity Day (Poush 27), Martyrs' Day (Magh 16) and Democracy Day (Falgun 7).

The Five Days in Detail

National Unity Day: Poush 27

Observed on the birth anniversary of Prithvi Narayan Shah, the king of Gorkha who began the unification of Nepal. Also called Prithvi Jayanti. See the Prithvi Jayanti 2083 date.

Martyrs' Day: Magh 16

Remembers four people executed during the Rana period: Shukraraj Shastri, Dharma Bhakta Mathema, Gangalal Shrestha and Dashrath Chand. Wreaths are laid at memorials. See the Martyrs' Day 2083 date.

Democracy Day: Falgun 7

Marks the end of Rana rule on Falgun 7, 2007 BS (February 1951) and the start of democratic politics. See the Democracy Day 2083 date.

Republic Day: Jestha 15

Jestha 15, 2065 BS is 28 May 2008, when Nepal became a federal democratic republic after the Constituent Assembly ended the monarchy. Wikipedia notes that the holiday has been cut since 2019 and the day is marked with programmes at workplaces, while our holiday list still shows it, so check your office or school notice.

National Day: Ashoj 3

The day the current Constitution was promulgated, on 20 September 2015. It is the official National Day by Cabinet decision. Read the full story on our National Day of Nepal page.

Which one is "the" national day? The Cabinet decided in 2016 that 20 September (Ashoj 3) is National Day. An earlier government had picked 28 May, the date Nepal became a republic, according to the Kathmandu Post. The other four are national days of remembrance or celebration but are not "the" National Day.

Are They Public Holidays?

Prithvi Jayanti, Martyrs' Day, Democracy Day and Constitution Day are national holidays in the 2083 holiday list. In 2083, Martyrs' Day (Saturday, 30 January 2027) falls on a weekly holiday. See the full Nepal public holidays 2083 list, and the BS to AD converter to check any date.

✦ Did you know?

  • All five days follow the Bikram Sambat calendar, so their English dates shift by a day between years.
  • The national flag, anthem and emblem are fixed by the Constitution, but none of these national days is written into it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the national days of Nepal?

Republic Day (Jestha 15), National Day (Ashoj 3), National Unity Day (Poush 27), Martyrs' Day (Magh 16) and Democracy Day (Falgun 7).

Which is the national day of Nepal?

National Day is 20 September (Ashoj 3), the day the Constitution was promulgated in 2015, as decided by the Cabinet in 2016.

When is Republic Day in Nepal?

Republic Day is on Jestha 15 each year. Jestha 15, 2084 BS is Saturday, 29 May 2027.

When is Democracy Day in Nepal?

Democracy Day is on Falgun 7. In 2083 BS it is Friday, 19 February 2027.

When is Martyrs' Day in Nepal?

Martyrs' Day is on Magh 16. In 2083 BS it is Saturday, 30 January 2027.

When is National Unity Day (Prithvi Jayanti)?

Prithvi Jayanti is on Poush 27. In 2083 BS it is Monday, 11 January 2027.

Who are the four martyrs of Nepal?

Shukraraj Shastri, Dharma Bhakta Mathema, Gangalal Shrestha and Dashrath Chand, remembered on Magh 16.

Why do national days change dates in English?

They follow the Bikram Sambat calendar, whose months do not match English months exactly, so the English date can shift by a day.
