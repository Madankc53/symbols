Frequently Asked Questions

How many questions are in the Nepal national symbols quiz?

Each round has 12 questions drawn at random from a bank of 20, so every attempt is a little different.

Is this quiz useful for Lok Sewa (PSC) preparation?

It covers general-knowledge facts on Nepal's symbols, with the constitutional articles where they apply. Always check the current syllabus and official sources for your exam.

Where do the answers come from?

From the guides on this site, which cite the Constitution of Nepal (2015) and government announcements. See the Official vs Unofficial page for the legal basis of each symbol.

Can I download a certificate?

At the end you can type your name to see it on the certificate and share your score. Taking a screenshot saves the certificate.
