National Day of Nepal

Nepal's National Day is 20 September (Ashoj 3), the day the 2015 Constitution was promulgated. It is observed as Constitution Day and National Day.

Nepal's National Day is the day the country's current Constitution was promulgated: 20 September 2015, which is Ashoj 3, 2072 BS. The government now observes it every year as Constitution Day and National Day (संविधान दिवस तथा राष्ट्रिय दिवस).

When Is National Day?

Ashoj 3 every year. Because Nepal follows the Bikram Sambat calendar, the English date is 19 or 20 September. In 2025 (Ashoj 3, 2082) it was marked on 19 September. By the calendar tables used on thenepal.io, Ashoj 3, 2083 is Saturday, 19 September 2026 and Ashoj 3, 2084 is Sunday, 19 September 2027.

Source: Constitution promulgation date (Ashoj 3, 2072 / 20 September 2015) as reported by nepsetrading.com, 19 September 2025; BS to AD conversion from our own BS to AD converter.

How 20 September Became National Day

Nepal's National Day was changed more than once. According to the Kathmandu Post, an earlier government had set 28 May as National Day. On 29 April 2016 the Cabinet cancelled that decision and declared 20 September, the day the Constituent Assembly's Constitution was promulgated, as National Day. The government also asked Nepal's embassies abroad to hold National Day events each year. The proposal went through the Foreign Ministry rather than the Home Ministry, which normally handles such matters.

Not written in the Constitution. National Day is a Cabinet decision. The Constitution of Nepal names the flag, anthem, emblem, colour, flower, animal and bird in Articles 6 to 9, but it does not set a National Day. We found no later decision that changes the 2016 one.

Source: The Kathmandu Post, "Govt decides to observe National Day on Sept 20" (16 May 2016). Decision text not seen.

How It Is Observed

The main state ceremony is held in Kathmandu. In 2025 it took place at the Army Pavilion (Sainik Manch) at Tundikhel with the President as chief guest and the Prime Minister addressing it. Cultural programmes such as tableaux and processions are usually part of the day, though they were suspended in 2025 because of the political situation. Nepali embassies abroad also mark the day.

Is It a Public Holiday?

Yes. Ashoj 3 (Constitution Day) is a national holiday in the 2083 public holiday list. In 2083 it falls on a Saturday, so it overlaps with the weekly holiday. See the 2083 public holidays list.

✦ Did you know?

  • The Constitution was promulgated on a Sunday, 20 September 2015, after years of drafting by two Constituent Assemblies.
  • National Day is separate from the official symbols: the flag, anthem and emblem are fixed by the Constitution, but the date of National Day is only a Cabinet decision.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the national day of Nepal?

Nepal's National Day is 20 September (Ashoj 3), the day the Constitution was promulgated in 2015. It is observed as Constitution Day and National Day.

Why is 20 September Nepal's national day?

It marks the promulgation of Nepal's current Constitution on 20 September 2015 (Ashoj 3, 2072). The Cabinet declared it National Day on 29 April 2016.

Is Constitution Day the same as National Day?

They are observed together. The government marks Ashoj 3 as Constitution Day and National Day.

What was Nepal's national day before 20 September?

According to the Kathmandu Post, an earlier government had chosen 28 May. The Cabinet cancelled that decision in 2016.

Is Prithvi Jayanti the national day of Nepal?

No. Prithvi Jayanti (Poush 27) is observed as National Unity Day. National Day is Ashoj 3.

Is National Day written in the Constitution?

No. It is a Cabinet decision, not an article of the Constitution.

Is National Day a public holiday in Nepal?

Yes, Ashoj 3 (Constitution Day) is a national holiday in the 2083 holiday list.
