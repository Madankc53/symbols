Nepal has four UNESCO World Heritage Sites: two natural (Sagarmatha National Park and Chitwan National Park) and two cultural (the Kathmandu Valley and Lumbini, the birthplace of the Buddha).

What Is Inside the Kathmandu Valley Site?

The Kathmandu Valley listing covers seven monument zones: the Durbar Squares of Kathmandu (Hanuman Dhoka), Patan and Bhaktapur, the Buddhist stupas of Swayambhunath and Boudhanath, and the Hindu temples of Pashupatinath and Changu Narayan.

Kathmandu Durbar SquarePatan Durbar SquareBhaktapur Durbar SquareSwayambhunathBoudhanathPashupatinathChangu Narayan
It was once on the "in danger" list. The Kathmandu Valley was on UNESCO's List of World Heritage in Danger from 2003 to 2007. We have not checked its current status, so see UNESCO for the latest.

What Is Not on the List

Famous places such as the Dharahara tower are not separate World Heritage Sites. Pashupatinath is part of the Kathmandu Valley listing. Nepal also keeps a longer "tentative list" of places that may be proposed later.

✦ Did you know?

  • Sagarmatha National Park protects the area around Mount Everest. Read more on our Mount Everest page.
  • Lumbini is where Siddhartha Gautama, the Buddha, was born.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many UNESCO World Heritage Sites are in Nepal?

Nepal has four: Sagarmatha National Park (1979), the Kathmandu Valley (1979), Chitwan National Park (1984) and Lumbini (1997).

What are the World Heritage Sites of Nepal?

Sagarmatha National Park, Kathmandu Valley, Chitwan National Park and Lumbini, the birthplace of Lord Buddha.

Which World Heritage Sites in Nepal are natural?

Sagarmatha National Park and Chitwan National Park are natural sites. The Kathmandu Valley and Lumbini are cultural sites.

What does the Kathmandu Valley World Heritage Site include?

Seven monument zones: the Durbar Squares of Kathmandu, Patan and Bhaktapur, Swayambhunath, Boudhanath, Pashupatinath and Changu Narayan.

When was Lumbini listed as a World Heritage Site?

Lumbini was inscribed in 1997.

Was the Kathmandu Valley ever on the danger list?

Yes, from 2003 to 2007.
